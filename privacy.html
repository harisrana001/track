Privacy Policy

App: Trace (com.cat.trace) • Effective Date: November 18, 2026

In short: Trace is designed to be offline-first and privacy-respecting. No account or login is required, your game progress stays on your device, anonymous usage analytics are off by default, and ads are limited to optional rewarded video hints via Google AdMob.

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how Trace (package ID com.cat.trace, developed by Blixora) handles information when you play our mobile application or visit this website.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Trace does not require registration, accounts, emails, usernames, or cloud logins. All gameplay progress is stored strictly on your local device using standard OS local storage (SharedPreferences):

  • Completed levels, star ratings, attempt counts, and daily streak history
  • Your hint wallet balance, daily free gift timestamps, and streak bonuses
  • Preferences such as sound effects, haptics, timer visibility, and reminder settings

This local data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use your phone's share sheet to share a spoiler-free victory card.

3. Rewarded Advertising (Google AdMob)

Trace uses Google AdMob exclusively to show optional, user-initiated Rewarded Ads when you choose to watch a video ad to use or refill puzzle hints. Trace does not display banner ads or forced interstitial ads.

When you choose to load or watch a rewarded ad, Google AdMob (Google LLC) may collect and process technical information necessary to serve, measure, and prevent fraud on ads—such as IP address, device type, operating system version, app interactions, diagnostics, and (subject to your device and regional consent settings) advertising identifiers.

  • Learn how Google uses data from apps using its services: Google Partner Sites Policy
  • Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Manage ad personalization on Android via Settings > Google > Ads or on iOS via Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

4. Optional Opt-In Anonymous Analytics

Anonymous gameplay analytics (via Google Firebase Analytics, when configured) are completely disabled by default at the native application level (including Advertising ID and ad personalization signal collection).

Only if you explicitly enable “Share anonymous usage data” in the app's Settings screen may Trace send minimal, sanitized, non-personal gameplay metrics to help tune puzzle difficulty:

  • Tutorial completion, level number (1–364), pack ID, star rating, reset count, and whether a hint was used
  • Broad duration buckets (<30s, 30-120s, 120-300s, >300s) and level brackets (e.g., 1-10, 11-50)
  • We never collect names, emails, location data, contacts, free-form text, or your exact puzzle solution moves.

You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings > About > Share anonymous usage data, which immediately stops analytics collection.

5. Local Notifications

If you optionally turn on daily puzzle reminders in Settings, notifications are scheduled entirely on your device using local OS alarms. No push notification tokens or personal identifiers are sent to any external server.

6. Children’s Privacy

Trace is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us via email or support channels, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because Trace stores game progress locally on your device, you can delete all locally saved game data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or uninstalling Trace. For data processed by Google AdMob in connection with rewarded ads, please refer to Google's retention policies linked in Section 3.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised Effective Date.
